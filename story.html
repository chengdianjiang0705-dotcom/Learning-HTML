The story of Nezha

A short introduction to a familiar figure from Chinese mythology.

A remarkable child

In the novel Fengshen Yanyi, Nezha is the son of Li Jing and Lady Yin. He has unusual abilities from birth and becomes a student of the immortal Taiyi Zhenren.

The lotus body

After conflict with the Dragon King's family, Nezha's story takes a tragic turn. Taiyi later brings him back to life with a body made from lotus roots.

Familiar objects

Nezha is often recognised by his Wind Fire Wheels, a fire-tipped spear, a golden ring, and a red sash. These objects make him easy to recognise in pictures and performances.

Different versions

Novels, films, and other retellings present Nezha differently. This page gives only a brief introduction; a modern adaptation may change parts of the older story.
