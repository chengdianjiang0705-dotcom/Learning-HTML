About this project

A small website for learning HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript.

Nezha Story Notes is a simple introduction to Nezha, a character from Chinese mythology. It brings together a short story summary and memorable film quotes about courage, family, and choosing your own path.

The website is for readers who are curious about Nezha and want an easy place to begin. It uses short English passages rather than a detailed account of every legend or film. The Quotes page keeps the original four film entries and adds eleven English excerpts from the older novel, with speakers, chapter numbers, and source links.

The five pages are Home, Story, Quotes, Project, and Machine. HTML structures the content, CSS provides the colours and layout, and shared JavaScript adds the same navigation and footer to each page. Small scripts also choose a random quotation and run two playful interactions, without accounts or extra libraries.

The project was created by Diancheng Jiang as a beginner web exercise. AI assisted with the wording and code revision. The portrait was supplied with the original exercise files; its original creator and reuse terms have not been identified.

Five project ideas

Five possible directions for a beginner website.

01 · Nezha Story Notes — chosen direction
A short guide for readers new to Nezha: a character introduction, story notes, quotations, and a playful machine. This direction connects a clear visual theme with manageable HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
02 · Chinese Mythology Starter
An introduction to five mythological characters, with short biographies, recognisable objects, and links to further reading. The audience would be beginners exploring Chinese stories.
03 · A Small Film Journal
A personal collection of five film reviews, organised by story, visual style, and one memorable scene. It would help classmates choose something to watch.
04 · Objects with a Story
A visual guide to legendary objects such as the Wind Fire Wheels and the Universe Ring. Each object would have a picture, a short explanation, and its place in the story.
05 · Everyday Courage
A collection of sourced quotations and short reflections about courage, responsibility, and learning from mistakes, with a random quotation on each visit.
